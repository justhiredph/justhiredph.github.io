🔒 Data Privacy Notice

How we collect, use, share and protect your personal information, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

In short

  • We use your details only to help you get hired, keep you updated, and pay any bonus or referral reward you earn.
  • We share your application only with the partner company you apply to. We never sell your data.
  • If a friend referred you, they see how your application is going (for example "interview set" or "hired"), never your personal details.
  • You can ask us anytime to see, correct or delete your data: joinus@justhiredhr.com.

Version: v1 · Effective 5 November 2026

1. Who we are

JustHired Philippines ("JustHired", "we", "us") is a recruitment agency in Makati City that helps applicants get hired by partner call center companies. We are responsible for the personal information you give us through our website (jobs.justhiredhr.com), our Facebook Messenger chat, email and phone.

2. What we collect

About youFull name, mobile number, email, date of birth, gender, civil status, home address or area
For job matchingEducation, school and course, work experience (including call center experience and past employers), preferred site and start date, expected salary, and anything else you tell us in your application
Your applicationInterview schedule, results and status, and screenshots or proof you send us for a company's application step
Bonuses and rewardsYour GCash number and the name on your GCash account
ReferralsWho referred you (their mobile number), or the friends you refer
Chats and feedbackYour Messenger name and ID, your messages to us, and suggestions you send through Usap Tayo
Website basicsOur pages remember your sign-in and progress on your own device. We use Google reCAPTCHA to block spam and bots.

3. Why we use it

  • To match you with open jobs and endorse you to the partner company you choose
  • To schedule your interview and send reminders, your Interview Pass and preparation tips
  • To follow up on your application, your first days at work and your 60 days, and to pay any joining bonus or referral reward you earn
  • To run JustHired Barkada (our referral program), including showing referrers how their friends' applications are going
  • To answer your questions and improve our service using your feedback
  • To keep our records accurate and to comply with the law

We use your information based on your consent, which you give when you tick the box on our website or tap "Yes, I agree" in Messenger, and as needed to deliver the service you asked for and to meet legal requirements. We keep a record of when and how you gave your consent.

4. Who we share it with

  • The partner company you apply to: your application details, so they can interview and hire you. Each company handles your data under its own privacy policy.
  • Your referrer (only if someone referred you): your first name with your last initial, and your application stage. Never your contact details, documents or other personal information.
  • Service providers that run our tools: Google (email, files and our records), Meta (Facebook Messenger), ManyChat (our chat assistant), GitHub (our website) and GCash (to send your payouts). They process data for us and only as needed for these services.
  • Government authorities, only when the law requires it.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your application or referrals are active, and for up to 3 years after your last activity with us so we can help you apply again and settle any bonus or reward. Payment records may be kept longer if the law requires it. After that, we delete or anonymize your information.

6. How we protect it

Your records are stored in private, password-protected business accounts that only authorized JustHired staff can open. Sign-in to the Barkada Hub uses a one-time code sent to your email, and we limit wrong attempts. Our team handles your information confidentially.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is used (this notice)
  • Access your data and get a copy of it
  • Correct wrong or outdated information
  • Object to or withdraw your consent. This stops future use, but we may not be able to continue helping with your application.
  • Ask us to delete or block your data
  • Be compensated for damages if your data is misused
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission

To use any of these rights, email us or message us on Messenger. We will reply as soon as we can.

8. Age

Our services are for applicants who are 18 years old and above. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger.
